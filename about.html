Who We Are

We are a non-profit organisation and public benefit organisation based in Uitenhage, Eastern Cape, South Africa.

We are dedicated to holistic development and empowerment of young people through our Information and Support Centre.

Executive Summary

Ekhaya Skills Initiative is a registered non-profit organisation from Kwa-Langa, ward 50 at Nelson Mandela Bay Metropolitan Municipality.

The organisation focuses on youth development, empowerment, social awareness and mentorship programmes.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to contribute positively towards the advancement of humanity through knowledge-based solutions to economic and social challenges.

Vision Statement

Our vision is to be a first-class centre of excellence in advancing information, training and development for needy communities in Nelson Mandela Bay.
