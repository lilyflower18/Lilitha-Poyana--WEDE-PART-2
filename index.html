About Us

Ekhaya Skills Initiative is a registered non-profit and public benefit organisation based in Uitenhage, Eastern Cape, South Africa. Founded in 2014 by unemployed youth from Kwa-Langa, the organisation was created to address the lack of opportunities and support available to young people in the community.

The initiative focuses on holistic youth development through its Information and Support Centre, where young people gain access to mentorship, career guidance, social awareness programmes, skills development and community support.

Ekhaya Skills Initiative believes in building a safer, empowered and informed community by helping youth stay away from crime and participate in positive activities that improve their future.
