"Unleashing Great Potential Through Skills Development"

Youth Development

Empowering young people through skills development, mentorship programmes and community support initiatives.

Programmes

• Pilot Information and Support Centre at Limekhaya High School.

• Life Skills & Mentorship Programme at local primary schools.

• Arts Wing made up of 25 youth artists.

• Feeding Programme supporting children and elderly people.

• Community awareness campaigns and motivational speaking.

• Multimedia services including photography and sound hire.

Beneficiaries

Our beneficiaries include unemployed youth, learners, vulnerable families, homeless children and community members in need of support and access to opportunities.

Dedicated volunteers also assist in youth and community development through partnerships and stakeholder networks.
